Step by step

From sign-up to first sale.

1

Register & choose a plan

Sign up with your email and pick a plan. Every plan starts with 14 days completely free — no card required.

  • Name your business
  • Choose Starter, Pro or Enterprise
  • You land in your dashboard
⏱ About 2 minutes
2

Customise your store

Open the visual editor and click on anything to change it — logo, colours, text, sections.

  • Upload your logo
  • Pick your brand colours
  • Edit headings and banners by clicking
⏱ About 5 minutes
3

Add your products

Add products one by one, scan barcodes, or bulk-import your whole catalogue from a spreadsheet.

  • Import CSV, Excel, JSON and more
  • Fix mistakes in the preview before importing
  • Set stock levels and low-stock alerts
⏱ 2–30 minutes
4

Connect payments

Link your Paystack account for card payments and add your bank details for transfers. Add POS terminals if you use them.

  • Paystack keys in Settings
  • Bank transfer details at checkout
  • Optional: OPay, Kuda, Moniepoint, PalmPay terminals
⏱ About 5 minutes
5

Start selling

Share your store link or your own domain. Use the POS for walk-in customers and watch the dashboard fill up.

  • Share on WhatsApp and Instagram
  • Ring up sales at the till
  • Track profit and stock live
⏱ Today
