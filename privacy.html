1. Who we are

QuickCart Nigeria (“QuickCart”, “we”) provides an online store, POS, inventory and accounting platform for businesses. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Contact: hnrkane@gmail.com.

2. Two kinds of people

  • Business owners and staff who sign up for QuickCart. For your account data, we are the data controller.
  • Shoppers and customers of a QuickCart store. The store owner decides how their data is used (controller); QuickCart only processes it on the owner’s behalf (processor). If you are a shopper and want to see or delete your data, contact the store first.

3. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, business name, password (stored only as a one-way scrypt hash), plan and billing status.
  • Store data you enter: products, prices, stock, orders, customer details, staff records, receipts and settings.
  • Payment references: Paystack transaction references and amounts. We do not see or store full card numbers.
  • Messages you send through the Contact page.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and request logs used to keep the Service secure and to limit repeated login attempts.

4. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and provide the Service (performance of a contract).
  • To process subscription payments and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the Service safe — for example blocking repeated failed logins.
  • To send essential emails such as password resets and receipts.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not run advertising trackers.

5. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data for us under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase — database hosting for accounts and store data.
  • Vercel — website and server hosting.
  • Paystack — card, bank and USSD payments.
  • Resend or Brevo — sending emails.

Some of these providers are located outside Nigeria, so your data may be transferred internationally. We only use providers that apply appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose data when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Cookies and similar storage

QuickCart uses only essential browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to keep you signed in, remember your store and speed up pages, plus a service worker for offline mode. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account and store data while your account is active. After you close it or it lapses, we keep data for a reasonable period (at least 30 days) so you can come back or export it, then delete it unless the law requires us to keep something longer. Security and rate-limit logs are kept only briefly.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; sessions use signed, expiring tokens; secrets such as bank-terminal keys are encrypted at rest; access to each store’s data is restricted to that store’s owner and staff; and traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password and tell us quickly if you suspect a problem.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to: access your personal data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to some processing; receive a portable copy; and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email hnrkane@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will show the new date at the top. For important changes we will notify you by email or in the dashboard.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests? Email hnrkane@gmail.com or use the Contact page.
